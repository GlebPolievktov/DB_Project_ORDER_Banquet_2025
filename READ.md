### Description of the subject area

In the restaurant, managers accept pre-orders for the organization of banquets. The following data is stored about managers in the database: unique number, passport data, date of employment. There is also a termination date, which is not filled in for current managers.

The restaurant has several halls for organizing banquets.Each room has a unique number and name. The maximum number of seats in the hall is also known.
The restaurant has a menu. The menu indicates the cipher of the dish, the name of the dish, the price, and the weight in grams.

When placing an order, the manager serving the banquet, the date, and the number of participants are indicated. One of the available halls is selected.

Each order is accompanied by a list of dishes selected from the menu and their quantity.
After compiling the list of dishes, the cost is calculated and an advance payment is made, the amount of which is saved in the order.
